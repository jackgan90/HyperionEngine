## Runtime scale stability repair

Two independent causes were reproduced before correction:

- Holding the absolute-position scale slider at a fixed mouse coordinate changed its own width every frame. The recorded sequence converged to a permanent 1.69, 1.70, 1.69, 1.70 cycle. Shared `FGui::ApplicationScaleControl` now uses bounded relative numeric dragging and Ctrl-click input in both Editor and Viewer, preserving live updates without geometry feedback.
- DockSpace consumed ImGui's preceding-frame WorkPos/WorkSize after the bars had already applied the new scale. In the reproduction, switching to 2.0 gave content Y bounds 96–918 on the first frame and 134–892 on the next. The private docking adapter now publishes current BuildWorkInset reservations before building the dock space. The bar edges match immediately. Normal automatic scrollbar visibility can settle from previous content size; it is separately checked for consecutive-frame stability.
