## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Scene-bound builtin inputs have one authoritative source
For a frame bound to a logical scene publication, builtin main directional direction/color and ambient color SHALL be generated by Renderer from scene light nodes, and builtin View camera position/projection SHALL be generated from the selected scene camera and View request. Conflicting external writes or custom providers for these protected canonical semantics SHALL be rejected before scene rendering. Existing custom semantics, supported material override policies and unbound explicit-view/provider APIs SHALL retain their documented behavior. The standard HyperionViewV1 and HyperionSceneV1 ABI layouts and builtin semantic names SHALL remain unchanged.

#### Scenario: Application attempts to replace a scene light
- **WHEN** SetSceneParameters receives a protected builtin light semantic while a logical scene is attached
- **THEN** the batch fails without changing current inputs and the client is directed to edit the scene light

#### Scenario: Custom provider conflicts with scene ownership
- **WHEN** a scene frame is frozen with a custom provider registered for a protected camera or light semantic
- **THEN** scene-frame admission fails with the conflicting semantic instead of allowing builtin rendering paths to disagree

#### Scenario: Unbound custom material fixture
- **WHEN** a low-level client without a logical scene uses explicit views and custom providers
- **THEN** existing generic provider precedence and scope validation continue to work

### Requirement: Resolved scene frames separate provenance from cache dependencies
Scene-bound material frames SHALL preserve scene publication provenance while deriving Scene and View scope revisions from their actual effective dependencies. Logical camera or metadata edits SHALL NOT by themselves invalidate unchanged geometry, object/material values, Scene lighting constants or resource identities. All views and deferred/forward/shadow consumers in the frame SHALL own immutable resolved values; an unresolved scene-frame seed SHALL NOT be accepted for material preparation.

#### Scenario: Camera motion with unchanged lights
- **WHEN** a scene camera moves while light, geometry, material and resource values remain unchanged
- **THEN** current view-dependent values update while unrelated Scene/Object/Material constants and compatible local preparation remain reusable

#### Scenario: Light intensity with unchanged geometry
- **WHEN** the selected light intensity changes
- **THEN** effective scene lighting values update without republishing model geometry, rebuilding the geometry BVH or replacing unchanged descriptor resources

#### Scenario: Resolve before deferred work
- **WHEN** a frame's native preparation is delayed while a later scene publication changes light and camera state
- **THEN** the delayed frame uses its original resolved parameters, constants and camera-dependent matrices
